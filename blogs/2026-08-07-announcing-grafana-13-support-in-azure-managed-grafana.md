Enhanced Dashboarding and Visualization Experience Grafana 13 introduces a number of improvements that make dashboards easier to build, reuse, and manage at scale. Teams can create richer observability experiences while reducing duplication and improving consistency across environments. These improvements include Dynamic Dashboards, Saved Queries, enhanced filtering and grouping experiences, dashboard templates, and additional usability enhancements that streamline dashboard authoring and discovery.
