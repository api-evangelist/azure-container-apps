Last week we announced the general availability of TLS/SSL certificate support and end-to-end TLS encryption for Azure Functions Flex Consumption. Flex Consumption is the recommended serverless hosting plan for Azure Functions. It combines event-driven scale-to-zero and execution-based billing with fast scale-out, configurable instance memory, always-ready instances, and virtual network integration.
