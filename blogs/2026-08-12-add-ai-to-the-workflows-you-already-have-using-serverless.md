There are a lot of ways to build with AI right now: chat frontends, copilots, greenfield agent apps, orchestration frameworks. All of them have their place, and some customers are building entirely new applications this way. But across customer engagements, a consistent pattern has emerged: the most successful and most cost-effective AI projects are not rewrites.
