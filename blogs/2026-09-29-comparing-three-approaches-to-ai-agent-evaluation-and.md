Why evaluation and observability belong together Agent evaluation and observability answer two related questions: Is the agent producing the right result? What happened from the initial request through every model and tool call? Evaluation measures quality with repeatable scores (is our system working), such as task completion, tool-call accuracy, groundedness, or safety.
